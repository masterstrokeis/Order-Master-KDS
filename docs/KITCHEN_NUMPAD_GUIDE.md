# Kitchen keypad — how to run the board without touching the screen

Use the **numeric keypad** (the square pad of number keys on the right).  
You do **not** need Num Lock. Laptop letter keys also work for numbers, Enter, minus, period, and slash.

Watch three things on the screen:

1. **The bar along the bottom** — it always tells you what each key does *right now*.
2. **The small pill** near the bottom of the board — it shows the number you are typing (`Order 18_`) or a short message (`No order #18`).
3. **The blue outline** on a ticket — while it is showing, **+** and **−** move between tickets. With no outline, they change tabs.

If you type the wrong number, press **`.`** (the decimal key) once and start again.

---

## The keys, in plain language

| Key | What it does |
|-----|----------------|
| **0–9** | Type a ticket number, an item number, or a list number (see below). |
| **Enter** | Confirm / start / complete / mark an item — whatever the bottom bar says. On a board with **no blue outline**, it grabs the next ticket to cook. |
| **+** and **−** | When a ticket has the **blue outline**: jump to the next / previous ticket. With **no outline**: switch tabs (Cooking → Completed → Cancelled). On the items list: scroll. |
| **/** | Jump to the **Items** list on the left, or back to the board. |
| **.** | Back out one step. Keep pressing until you are back to a normal board. |
| **\*** | Undo a **completed** ticket (send it back to Cooking). Only works when that ticket is selected. |

Do **not** hold a key down. One press, then wait.

---

## Everyday jobs

### Start or finish a whole ticket

1. Type the ticket number you see on the card (example: **18**).
2. Press **Enter**. The card gets a **blue outline** and item numbers appear on the left of each line.
3. Press **Enter** again:
   - New ticket → **Start**
   - Cooking ticket → **Complete** (moves it off Cooking)

The board scrolls that ticket into view if it was off to the side.

### See tickets hiding off the side of the screen

When the board is full, some tickets sit off to the right where you cannot see them — including tickets that have just arrived. You do **not** need to know their numbers.

1. Press **Enter** on a plain board (no blue outline anywhere). The outline lands on the **first ticket nobody has started yet** — which is where a ticket that just came in always sits. If every ticket is already cooking, it lands on the first ticket on the board (top left) instead. Leftovers from a previous day are skipped.
2. Press **+** to walk to the next ticket, **−** to walk back. The board slides along with you, so hidden tickets come into view one at a time.
3. When the pill says **End of board**, you have seen every ticket on this tab. **Start of board** means you are back at the first one.
4. Press **Enter** to start or complete the outlined ticket, or **.** to let it go.

Remember: while the outline is showing, **+** and **−** move between tickets. Once the outline is gone, they change tabs. The bottom bar always tells you which one you will get.

Two small things:

- If you had started typing a number, **+** or **−** throws that number away and moves the outline. Finish typing and press **Enter** first if you meant to jump to a ticket.
- Walking can land you on a **leftover** ticket from a previous day. The pill says `Use Clear (touch)` — tap **Clear** on that card, then carry on with **+**.

### Mark one line on a ticket (start cooking that item)

1. Type the ticket number, **Enter** (blue outline).
2. Type the **item number** printed in the blue column (1, 2, 3…).
3. Press **Enter**.
   - On a **new** ticket this also **starts** the ticket and marks that line done.
   - On a **cooking** ticket this ticks the line on/off (same as a double-tap).

### Undo a finished ticket

1. Press **.** until no ticket has a blue outline, then press **+** until you are on the **Completed** tab.
2. Type the ticket number, **Enter**.
3. Press **\***. The ticket goes back to Cooking.

### Work from the Items list (left side)

1. Press **/**. The list gets a blue outline and numbers on each product.
2. Type the product number, **Enter**. The breakdown panel opens.
3. Then either:
   - Type a **line number** and **Enter** to complete that one order’s portion, or
   - Press **Enter** with nothing typed to **Complete all**.
4. Press **.** to close the panel. Press **.** again (or **/**) to go back to the board.

If the Items list is turned off in Settings, **/** does nothing and the pill says **Items list hidden**.

### Change tabs without touching the screen

On the **board**, with **no blue outline** showing:

- **+** → Cooking → Completed → Cancelled → Cooking…
- **−** → the other way

The pill names the tab you land on (**Completed tab**), so if you change tab by mistake, press the other key once to come straight back.

If a ticket has the blue outline, **+** and **−** walk between tickets instead. Press **.** to drop the outline first, then change tabs.

## What **`.`** does (always “go back”)

Each press undoes **one** thing:

1. Clears the number you were typing (or a warning message).
2. Closes the breakdown panel, if it is open.
3. Leaves the Items list and returns to the board.
4. Drops the blue outline on a ticket.

Keep pressing **`.`** until the outline is gone and you are looking at the board. That is the safe “I’m lost” key.

---

## What you will see

| On screen | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Blue outline on a ticket | That ticket is selected. Numbers on the lines are live. |
| Blue outline on the Items list | You are working the list, not the board. |
| `Order 18_` | You are typing ticket **18**. Press Enter to select it. |
| `Item 3_` | That ticket is already selected; you are typing line **3**. |
| `No order #18` | No ticket with that number on **this tab**. Check Cooking / Completed / Cancelled. |
| `No item 7` | That ticket does not have a 7th line. |
| `End of board` / `Start of board` | You have walked to the last / first ticket on this tab. The outline stays where it is. |
| `Cooking tab` / `Completed tab` / `Cancelled tab` | You just changed tab. Press the other key once to go back. |
| `Order closed` | You cannot change items on a completed or cancelled ticket. |
| `Use Clear (touch)` | Leftover from a previous day — tap **Clear** on the card. |
| `Already completed` / `Order cancelled` | Enter will not move that ticket further. |

## Quick “recipes”

**Bump a cooking ticket:** `18` → Enter → Enter  

**Find a ticket I can’t see (no number needed):** Enter → `+` `+` `+`  

**Fire one line on a new ticket:** `18` → Enter → `2` → Enter  

**Open “Burger” from the items list:** `/` → `1` → Enter  

**Complete every Burger on the panel:** `/` → number → Enter → Enter  

**I typed the wrong number:** `.` then type again  

**Get back to a calm board:** `.` `.` `.` until the blue outline is gone  

---

## Things the keypad does **not** do

- Change station / terminal (use the dropdown with the mouse or finger).
- Open Settings or log in.
- Answer the “new business day” pop-up (tap Clear or Cancel).
- Clear a leftover ticket from a previous day (tap **Clear** on the card).
- Slide the board on its own without picking a ticket. To reach hidden tickets, press Enter then walk with **+**, or tap the on-screen **More** buttons.

The bottom bar is the cheat sheet. If you forget a key, look there.
